Degree Name Different from Passport — Complete Solution Guide 2026

Updated September 2026 · Practical guide for HEC, MOFA, Mosadaqa & Embassy acceptance

One of the most common reasons documents get rejected during attestation in Pakistan is a name mismatch between the educational certificate (degree, transcript, diploma) and the passport or CNIC. This guide explains why it happens and exactly what you can do.

Why Name Mismatch Causes Rejection

HEC, IBCC, MOFA, Mosadaqa (Saudi Culture) and most embassies cross-check the name on the educational document with the name on the passport / CNIC. Even small differences can stop the process:

  • Spelling variations (Muhammad / Mohammad / Mohammed)
  • Missing or extra middle name
  • Different sequence of names
  • Father’s name written differently
  • Old certificates with incomplete personal details

What Options Do You Have?

1. Affidavit + Undertaking (Most Common First Step)

A properly worded affidavit stating that both names refer to the same person, usually sworn before a notary or magistrate, is often accepted as supporting evidence. Many embassies and MOFA accept this when the difference is minor (spelling or one missing name).

2. Gazette Notification

For more significant differences, publishing a name change / clarification in the official Gazette of Pakistan creates a legal record that authorities can rely on.

3. Re-issuance or Correction from Board / University

In some cases the board or university can issue a corrected certificate or a formal letter confirming the correct name. This is slower but the cleanest long-term solution.

4. Supporting Documents Bundle

Combining CNIC, passport, B-form, previous educational records and an affidavit often satisfies HEC and MOFA when the variation is consistent across older documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Mosadaqa (Saudi) accept an affidavit for name difference?

In many cases yes, when the difference is minor and properly documented. Severe differences may require additional steps. We check the latest requirements for each case.

Does Apostille also need name matching?

Yes. MOFA issues Apostille only after verifying the underlying document. Name consistency is still required.

How long does fixing a name issue take?

Simple affidavit cases can be prepared in 1–3 days. Gazette or re-issuance takes longer. We give realistic timelines after reviewing your documents.
