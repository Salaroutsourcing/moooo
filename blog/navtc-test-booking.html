How to Book NAVTC / Takamol Skill Test in Pakistan — Full Guide

Updated 2026 · Skill Verification Program (SVP) for Saudi Arabia

If you are a skilled worker going to Saudi Arabia, you may need to pass the NAVTTC Skill Verification Program (SVP), also known as the Takamol test. This guide covers what it is and how to get your appointment booked correctly.

What is the NAVTC / Takamol Test?

It is a skills assessment conducted under the partnership between Pakistan’s National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC) and Saudi Arabia’s Takamol. Certain trades (electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, and others) require this verification before visa processing.

What You Typically Need

  • Valid Passport
  • Original CNIC
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Any trade-related certificates (if available)
  • Payment for the official test fee + facilitation (if using a service)

Test Centers

Official centers operate in major cities including Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Gujranwala and others. Availability of slots varies by city and trade.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking the wrong trade or wrong center
  • Incomplete documents on test day
  • Missing the appointment window
  • Paying unofficial agents without confirmation
